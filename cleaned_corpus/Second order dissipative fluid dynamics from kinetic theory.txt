4 Conclusions
In this work we derived the equations of second order dissipative fluid dynamics with all first- and
second-ordertermsfromtheBoltzmannequationusingthe14-momentmethodwithalinearizedcol-
lision integral.We also expressedallcoefficientsmultiplyingthe secondordertermsindependentof
thechoiceofframeandshowedthatsomeofthesecoefficientsaredifferentindifferentframes.
