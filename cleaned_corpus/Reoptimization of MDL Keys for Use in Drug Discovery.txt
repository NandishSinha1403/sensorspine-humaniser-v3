of more than 1000 bits. Contrary to initial expectations, pruning on the basis of the surprisal values of the

keysets.Oncemoretheperformancewasonlyaweakfunctionofkeysetsize,andtheoptimizationsfailed
to identify a single globally optimal keyset. Instead multiple, equally optimal keysets could be produced
which had relatively low overlap of the descriptors they encoded.
